Offensive Security Analysis Report

Role: All

Skills: Research, Writing, Offensive Security

This security report serves as a formal log of the security analysis conducted on this content has been redacted from October 10, 2024 to November 15, 2024. Initially, we had proposed to our client a limited scope analysis such that we could adequately analyze the cyber operations of our client without interfering with their daily operations. Since our client operates in the critical infrastructure sector, minimizing interference with their operations was of the utmost importance in order to maintain the integrity of our client. Our initial proposal included five tests across four categories:

1) social engineering tests, including vishing and phishing simulations;
2) network security tests, such as an external domain review and network traffic monitoring;
3) access control review, including an active directory domain audit; and
4) static analysis, focusing on code and application review.

After consulting with our client, we agreed to conduct only the first three test categories, and excluded any static code review from the testing period. The reasons for this were primarily due to time and resource constraints, but we and our client also agreed that the first four tests were more integral to their operations at this time. In addition to conducting a limited scope analysis, we collaborated extensively with our client’s executives to design and implement each test. During the testing period, our approach primarily adhered to “white box” principles of offensive security, granting us access to detailed knowledge of the client’s infrastructure and internal operations.
